Privacy Policy

App: Day 75: Challenge Tracker · Developer: Sorencorp · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Sorencorp ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Day 75 Android application (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Your challenge data stays on your device

The App does not require an account and has no servers of its own. Your challenges, daily tasks, check-ins, history and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see, access or recover this data. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and general usage data. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Purchasing Day 75 Pro removes ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The App only stores, on your device, whether Pro is active.

4. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions? Write to daveloper0+sorencorp@gmail.com.
