Privacy Policy

App: MiCartera · Developer: SorenCorp · Last updated: March 22, 2026

1. Introduction

MiCartera ("the App") is a personal digital vault for managing documents, pets, and health information on Android, developed by SorenCorp. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use the App and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

MiCartera does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information to SorenCorp or external servers.

However, the App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data automatically:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads
  • Device information (model, OS version)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy.

3. Camera Permission

The App uses the CAMERA permission to allow you to scan and photograph your documents (ID cards, passports, licenses, etc.).

  • Photos are stored locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any server
  • Never accessed by SorenCorp
  • Deleted at your discretion

4. Local Storage

All data you enter into MiCartera is stored 100% locally on your device using SQLite database:

  • Documents (photos and metadata)
  • Pet information (name, breed, vaccinations)
  • Health data (appointments, medications, allergies, checkups)
  • Biometric authentication settings

This data:

  • Is never synchronized to the cloud
  • Is never shared with any third party
  • Is encrypted by your device's security
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App

5. Security

Access to your data is protected by:

  • 4-digit PIN (hashed with SHA-256, stored locally only)
  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition)
  • Device-level encryption (handled by Android)

We do not have access to your data at any time.

7. In-App Purchases

MiCartera offers an optional non-consumable purchase to remove advertisements ("Remove Ads").

  • Purchase is processed by Google Play Billing
  • Your payment information is handled by Google, not by SorenCorp
  • No personal health data is transmitted during purchase
  • The purchase status is stored locally on your device

8. Permissions Used

Permission Purpose Data Handling
CAMERA Scan documents with photo Stored locally, never transmitted
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Access gallery for photos Stored locally, never transmitted
USE_BIOMETRIC Fingerprint/face unlock Processed by Android, not stored by App
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Medical appointment reminders Stored locally via system alarms
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display appointment notifications Triggered locally on your device

9. Children's Privacy

MiCartera is designed for adults to manage their personal information. The App is rated 3+ because it does not contain inappropriate content, but it is not intended for children under 13.

10. Data Retention

MiCartera stores only the data you actively enter. All data is deleted when you manually delete an item, clear app data from device settings, or uninstall the App.

11. Your Rights

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the App
  • Deletion: You can delete any data from within the App
  • AdMob opt-out: https://adssettings.google.com
  • No data sales: We never sell, rent, or share your data

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

13. Compliance

MiCartera is designed to comply with GDPR, CCPA, and Android Privacy Requirements.
